# Why RAMX fits Agent Economy

AI agents are becoming customers, but most AI services still expect a human with a credit card and an account. RAMX lets an agent buy inference on its own: it calls an OpenAI-compatible API, receives an HTTP 402 with a price, signs a USDC payment on NEAR with x402, and gets its answer, with no sign-up, no subscription and no human in the loop. The gateway runs its own facilitator, so a relayer account submits the agent's signed transfer and pays the gas, and every payment lands on the ledger contract with a receipt the agent can check. On the other side of the market, laptop owners earn USDC for the answers their machines serve, paid each epoch through an on-chain Merkle payout. Agents pay machines, machines get paid, and nobody has to trust a middleman's spreadsheet: this is an agent economy running on real USDC on NEAR testnet, with live transactions ([G3rzbD…](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/G3rzbD1y6sEfAfJA79tH8aQmBYsj3jYDEEM5XphzNJY4), [2v7At7…](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/2v7At7Ez6x31TtsaZyntsBSPrwnuBVmoygSDPXgMgdth)) whose signed ledger entries anyone can verify.

An agent economy also needs limits, because an agent with a wallet can be tricked into spending it. RAMX gives owners two modes: an open agent pays per request with x402 under a budget cap, and a guarded agent holds only a NEAR function-call key that can call `escrow.pay` and nothing else. The escrow contract enforces the owner's policy on chain (allowed payees, a per-call maximum, a total budget and a pause switch), so a prompt-injected agent cannot pay a stranger, overspend or change its own limits; our attack demo shows five such attempts refused on chain, while a legitimate guarded payment goes through and gets its answer ([9gFGWr…](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/9gFGWr23xEkdqPXF97T5Y5FHNgP6caHTaqA7sEyRXaPh)). Owners can also hand agents API keys with credit caps, and in the capacity market 25 agents, each with its own NEAR account, bid for priority slots and their results are recorded on the ledger. Together this shows agents that can earn, spend and trade autonomously, and stay inside rules the chain enforces.
