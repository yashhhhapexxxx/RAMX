THE SOLUTION

07

Nearby laptops form a pool. Each one loads a slice of the model's layers.

A request flows through the slices in order, and the answer streams back. Mac and NVIDIA in the same pool.

A coordinator routes each request to the best pool and splits the payment by compute.

TECHNICAL WOW 02 · ROUTING

13
score = reliability × (1 − load)

reliability = 0.7 × success + 0.3 × uptime

The pool is the reputation. Requests it served over 7 days, plus heartbeat slots where every layer was covered. A new pool starts at 0.8.

load = running ÷ slots

Pools batch requests (2 at once today), so a pool at 1 of 2 slots is 50% loaded and still a candidate.
